INSPECTION REPORT

Plant: Rajahmundry Petrochemical Complex, Unit 3
Department: Quality & Safety Assurance
Inspector: Inspector: S. Venkatesh
Date: 02/20/2026

Equipment Inspected: TK-305 (Storage Tank), V-204 (Control Valve)

Finding: Observed that TK-305 external shell shows early-stage corrosion
near the base weld seam, approximately 1.2 meters from the foundation.
No active leakage was observed. Severity: medium.

Finding: Observed that V-204 actuator linkage has minor play, causing
inconsistent valve positioning under 8 bar line pressure. Severity: low.

Compliance Check:
TK-305 last hydro-tested 11 months ago; certificate valid per ISO 16961.
Safety data sheet for stored fluid (Ethylene Glycol) is posted at the
tank site as required.

Recommendation:
Schedule ultrasonic thickness testing on TK-305 base weld within 60 days.
Replace V-204 actuator linkage bushing during next planned shutdown.

Regulatory Reference: ISO 16961, OISD-118

Location: Unit 3, Tank Farm North

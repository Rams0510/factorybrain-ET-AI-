MAINTENANCE REPORT

Plant: Rajahmundry Petrochemical Complex, Unit 3
Department: Rotating Equipment Maintenance
Prepared by: Engineer: Arvind Rao
Date: 03/14/2026

Equipment: P-101 (Centrifugal Feed Pump)

Findings:
During routine inspection, P-101 exhibited excessive vibration and a
minor seal leakage at the mechanical seal housing. Discharge pressure
readings were logged at 12.4 bar, above the design maximum of 10 bar.
Bearing temperature was measured at 84 C, exceeding the safe operating
threshold of 75 C.

Failure Reason:
Bearing overheating caused by inadequate lubrication schedule combined
with elevated discharge pressure from a partially closed downstream
valve (V-204). Seal leakage is a secondary effect of shaft misalignment
induced by bearing wear.

Root Cause:
Lubrication interval was extended from monthly to bi-monthly six months
ago as a cost-saving measure without a corresponding vibration-monitoring
upgrade, allowing bearing degradation to go undetected.

Action Taken:
Replaced drive-end and non-drive-end bearings. Realigned pump-motor
coupling to within 0.05mm TIR. Replaced mechanical seal. Restored
lubrication interval to monthly and added a vibration sensor for
continuous monitoring.

Downtime: 14 hours

Safety Note:
Operators must not restart P-101 without confirming bearing temperature
is below 75 C and vibration readings are within ISO 10816 Zone B limits.

Regulatory Reference: OISD-118, ISO 10816

Location: Unit 3, Pump House B

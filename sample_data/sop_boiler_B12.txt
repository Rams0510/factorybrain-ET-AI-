STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

Title: SOP for Boiler B-12 Startup and Shutdown
Department: Utilities
Plant: Rajahmundry Petrochemical Complex, Unit 3
Prepared by: Engineer: Meena Iyer
Approved by: Supervisor: R. K. Sharma

1. Purpose
This SOP defines the mandatory steps for safe startup and shutdown of
Boiler B-12 (water-tube, 45 TPH capacity).

2. Pre-Startup Checks
Operators must verify that all drain valves are closed and the feedwater
level is between the low and high alarm setpoints. The safety valve set
pressure must be confirmed at 17.5 bar before firing.

3. Startup Sequence
Operators shall purge the furnace for a minimum of 5 minutes before
ignition. Steam drum pressure must be raised gradually, not exceeding
2 bar per 10 minutes, to avoid thermal stress on the drum shell.

4. Safety Requirements
Operators must never bypass the low-water cutoff interlock under any
circumstance. Personnel must wear appropriate PPE including insulated
gloves near the burner front. Boiler B-12 shall be inspected annually
per PESO regulations and Factory Act, 1948 requirements.

5. Shutdown Sequence
Reduce firing rate gradually. Maintain feedwater flow until drum
pressure drops below 2 bar. Do not open manhole covers until drum
temperature is below 60 C.

6. Regulatory References
This SOP complies with OISD-117, PESO CCE guidelines, and Factory Act,
1948, Section 28 (steam boilers).

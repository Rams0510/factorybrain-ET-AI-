INCIDENT REPORT

Plant: Rajahmundry Petrochemical Complex, Unit 3
Department: HSE
Prepared by: Engineer: Arvind Rao
Date: 01/09/2026

Equipment Involved: P-101 (Centrifugal Feed Pump)

Incident Summary:
A near miss occurred when a technician noticed unusual vibration and
a burning smell near P-101 during a routine walk-down. The pump was
immediately shut down before bearing seizure occurred.

Failure Reason:
Vibration was later attributed to bearing overheating consistent with
the lubrication interval issue identified in prior maintenance findings.

Lessons Learned:
Vibration monitoring should not rely solely on scheduled walk-downs.
Continuous online monitoring should be prioritized for critical rotating
equipment such as P-101.

Corrective Action:
Installed a permanent vibration sensor on P-101. Updated the near-miss
register and briefed the shift team.

Regulatory Reference: OISD-118

Location: Unit 3, Pump House B
